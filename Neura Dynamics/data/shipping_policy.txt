**Shipping Policy**

**1. Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates**
Shipping charges for your order will be calculated and displayed at checkout.
- Standard Shipping (5-7 business days): Free for orders over $50.
- Expedited Shipping (2-3 business days): $15.00.
- Overnight Delivery (1 business day): $35.00.

**2. Shipment Processing Time**
All orders are processed within **1-2 business days**. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or holidays. If we are experiencing a high volume of orders, shipments may be delayed by a few days.

**3. International Shipping**
We currently ship to select countries outside of the US. International shipping rates vary by location. Please note that customers are responsible for any customs, duties, and taxes applied to their order.

**4. Damages during Shipping**
We are not liable for any products damaged or lost during shipping. If you received your order damaged, please contact the shipment carrier to file a claim. Please save all packaging materials and damaged goods before filing a claim.
