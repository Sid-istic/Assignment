**Cancellation Policy**

**1. Order Cancellation**
You may cancel your order within **24 hours** of placement without any penalty. After 24 hours, if the order has not yet been shipped, a **10% cancellation fee** may apply to cover processing costs.

**2. Shipped Orders**
If your order has already been shipped, it cannot be canceled. You will need to wait for the item to arrive and then follow our Return & Refund Policy.

**3. Subscription Cancellations**
For subscription services, you may cancel at any time. Cancellations made after the billing cycle will remain active until the end of that cycle, and no pro-rated refund will be given for the remainder of the month.
